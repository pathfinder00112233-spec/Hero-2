Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my dental crown ever loosen or fall off while eating?

No. We use dual-cure adhesive resin cements that form a chemical micromechanical bond with the prepared tooth structure. Under normal chewing conditions, the crown stays locked in place.

What should I choose for a missing tooth: Dental Implant or Fixed Bridge?

A single dental implant is the clinical gold standard because it avoids touching the neighboring healthy teeth. If bone density is insufficient or surgery is contraindicated, a fixed zirconia bridge is the next best fixed alternative.

Can a tooth with a crown still develop cavities?

While the zirconia material itself cannot decay, the natural root margin where the crown meets the gum is still vulnerable to plaque. Daily brushing, flossing, and periodic cleanings prevent root decay.

How many appointments does getting a crown take?

Typically two short visits over 48 to 72 hours. Visit 1 is for tooth preparation, digital scanning, and placing a temporary crown. Visit 2 is for checking the contact fit and permanently cementing your CAD/CAM zirconia crown.
