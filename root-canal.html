Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the root canal procedure painful?

No. The procedure is performed under computerized local anesthesia. Patients feel zero pain during canal debridement. In fact, opening the canal relieves the severe throbbing pressure caused by infected pulp tissue.

Can I complete my root canal in a single visit?

Yes. Over 85% of cases are completed in a single 45-minute sitting using flexible rotary instruments. Only teeth with severe swelling or active pus drainage require a second sitting.

Is a crown always necessary after a root canal?

Yes. Once the tooth pulp is removed, the remaining enamel becomes brittle without internal hydration. Placing a monolithic zirconia crown restores chewing strength and prevents the tooth from cracking in half.

Why did my previous root canal at another clinic fail?

Canals can fail if complex curved root anatomy or hidden extra canals (such as the MB2 canal in molars) are missed with manual hand tools. Our high-magnification surgical lighting and rotary protocol ensure every canal is cleared and sealed.
