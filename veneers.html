Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my teeth going to be shaved into small pegs?

No. Aggressive tooth reduction is an outdated crown technique. Ultra-thin E-Max veneers only require 0.3mm to 0.5mm micro-buffing of the outermost enamel layer. More than 90% of your natural healthy tooth structure is preserved.

Can I bite into food normally with porcelain veneers?

Yes. Once bonded with adhesive resin cements, veneers fuse with natural enamel and act as a unified tooth surface. You can eat rotis, apples, and normal food without hesitation.

How many veneers are typically needed for a smile makeover?

Most patients receive 6 to 8 veneers across the upper anterior arch (canine to canine or premolar to premolar), which covers the full smile zone visible when laughing or speaking.

How many appointments does a smile makeover require?

Typically two appointments over 4 to 5 days. Visit 1 involves digital scanning, shade selection, and test-driving the provisional mockup. Visit 2 is for permanently bonding your custom ceramic veneers.
